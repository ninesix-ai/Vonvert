# First-run guide & role presets

Vonvert can tailor itself to how you plan to use it. On first launch a short guide asks
which role fits you best; the choice then tunes the interface and the starting voice.

## What the role picker does

- **Interface complexity** — the expert parameter panel opens in either **Simple** or
  **Professional** mode to match the role's default complexity level (Beginner starts
  minimal, Audio engineer starts fully exposed).
- **Recommended voices** — the built-in presets shown first are chosen for your role.
- The role never changes how your voice is actually processed — it only tailors what the
  interface exposes and suggests. You always keep full access to every control via the
  complexity override.

## The first-run guide

A four-step guide appears the first time you open Vonvert:

1. **Welcome** — a one-line overview.
2. **Role** — pick one of the roles, grouped under three headings: *Fun & gaming*,
   *Content creation*, and *Professional audio*.
3. **Devices** — choose your microphone and output device.
4. **Done** — you can start right away.

You can **Skip** at any time; skipping simply keeps the default interface. The guide runs
once and will not appear again after you finish or skip it.

## Changing your role later

Open **Settings → My role** to change the role or the interface-complexity level at any
time. Lowering the complexity hides advanced controls but never deletes parameters you
have already tuned.

## Privacy

The role system runs entirely on your machine. It collects no telemetry and shows no
upgrade prompts. Your role selection is stored only in your local settings file.
